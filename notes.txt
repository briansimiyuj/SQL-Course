To insert rows in a table; we use:
    `INSERT INTO $table_name
    VALUES ($column1, $column2, $column3, ...);`   

Ensure the columns match the data types

To insert multiple rows at once:
    `INSERT INTO $table_name
    VALUES ($column1, $column2, $column3, ...),
    ($column1, $column2, $column3, ...),
    ($column1, $column2, $column3, ...);`
