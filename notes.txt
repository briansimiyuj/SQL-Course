To get the current date; we use:
    `CURRENT_DATE()`

To get the current time; we use:
    `CURRENT_TIME()`

To get the current date and time; we use:
    `CURRENT_TIMESTAMP()`

To get the yesterday's date; we use:
    `CURRENT_DATE() - 1`

To get the tomorrow's date; we use:
    `CURRENT_DATE() + 1`
